# Diagnose and review

Record action + phase + view + chain + the user's defect. Compare front, side,
oblique and overhead at controlled slope/camera scale. Whole shafts and tips
matter; a narrower hand span can worsen the pole silhouette. Distinguish an
unjudgeable modeled neck shape from judgeable head/gaze orientation.

1. Find the first defective final frame and its neighbors. Form one causal
   hypothesis. Diagnose dominant source, requested and final poses: source good /
   requested bad suggests composition; requested good / final bad suggests
   tracking, limits or fitting. Diagnostic `present_authored` views are
   reconstructions; confirm against raw joints/rotations.
2. Change the owning stage with phase-shaped targets and smooth release. Preserve
   rest lengths, connected rotations and rigid equipment. Repeated target changes
   without final improvement require tracing the intervening constraint.
3. Capture a new candidate; inspect selected event frames, final measurements and
   selected clothing clearance. Reject impossible/worse candidates early.
4. Inspect every chronological frame through entry/hold/release and neighboring
   actions, then full relevant audit/regressions. R4 selected stills missed
   transition clipping. Compare identical ticks/inputs/physics and controlled
   cameras at normal/slow speed; inspect gameplay for runtime changes.

The clothing audit skins the actual mesh and tests a centre ray plus four offsets
at 6 mm radius, from 10 cm below the grip to the tip. It approximates shaft
clearance, not swept volume or finger contact. Preparation coverage normally ends
at support loss. Flight/landing work needs `-IncludeFlight`, an actual
`include_flight: true` receipt and nonzero coverage of each requested scenario.

Measure hand/elbow span, chest-relative depth, shaft flare and tip span together,
plus stance, spine/gaze, knee/cuff angle, attachment errors and continuity.
Carving/balance requires open arms; historical tuck thresholds are not universal.
Retained R1-R4 findings live in [Animation](../../../../docs/ANIMATION.md#retained-findings-and-acceptance).

When numeric grading is requested, assess every requested action/phase and
judgeable chain; an average cannot hide a failing wrist. Apply the requested
thresholds (the historical R4 request was overall >=8.0, no bone <6.5), not an
invented universal gate. Record reviewer, evidence ID, status, score and defect.
Unrated is incomplete; zero is a real score; unjudgeable requires an explicit
reason/exclusion. User/model records remain independent. A callable independent
critic must see the actual new evidence and rubric; otherwise label author review
and leave independent grading pending. Do not create an unsolicited task.

Optional synthetic references must match rig proportions, action phase and
straight full-length equipment. Save prompt/provenance, inspect consistency and
label them synthetic; they supply neither hidden 3D joints nor real timing.
